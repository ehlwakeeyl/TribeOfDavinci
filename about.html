About Clean Shelters

Cleaning should be professional. The experience should be effortless.

Clean Shelters Ltd is a modern cleaning company built for Abuja — serving homes, offices, shortlets and commercial spaces with a standard of care that’s consistent every time.

Who We Are

Built around a simple idea.

Clean Shelters Ltd exists to make professional cleaning straightforward — for homeowners who want their space cared for properly, and for businesses that need a cleaning partner they can rely on.

We work across residential, commercial and shortlet spaces in Abuja, bringing the same standard of reliability, professionalism and attention to detail to every job, whatever its size.

What Guides Us

Our mission. Our standard. Our approach.

Our Mission

To make professional cleaning accessible and dependable for every home, office and shortlet in Abuja — without cutting corners and without unnecessary premium pricing.

Our Standard

Every space is treated with the same level of care, whether it’s a one-bedroom apartment or a corporate office. We clean it properly, every time.
